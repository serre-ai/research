1. **Verification under optimization pressure** (VeriFine, ScienceClaw, Agent Bottling)

2. **Monitorability and real-time oversight** (Evidence-Bound Reasoning, SAFE-MR, Agent History Compaction)
   - When can we verify reasoning traces in real-time?

   - Safety-critical question for autonomous systems

3. **Agent calibration and over-defensiveness** (ParanoiaEval, Principled Under Pressure)
   - Agents over-invest in defensive work
   - Risk assessment is poorly calibrated
   - Efficiency-correctness tradeoff
